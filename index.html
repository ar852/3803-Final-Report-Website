Executive Summary

The National Weather Service is a United States government agency tasked with providing weather forecasts, hazard warnings, and other weather-related information to the public. Their official website, simply known as weather.gov, holds accurate weather data by the hour since 1912. With all this data being collected rapidly, they are also the most accurate source of weather information and forecasting in the nation. Almost all weather programs, be it the app on your smartphone or the Weather Channel, source their data from the National Weather Service.

Since weather.gov has an immense amount of information spanning numerous categories, the website is very complex to navigate. For an agency renowned with having the most accurate weather data in the country, it’s unfortunate that the average daily user finds the website cumbersome for daily use. The website overall has an outdated structure, low image and resolution quality, and requires far too much in-depth navigation to find certain information.

Our team wanted to re-design this website to make it more convenient, more organized, and most importantly, more efficient. While we still want the National Weather Service to be useful as a historical archive for researchers or scientists to closely examine data, we also wanted to make the website accessible to the average digital user, regardless of the device they have, so they can find accurate weather information they might need immediately and easily.
